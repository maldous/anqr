## Key Design Decisions

1. **Vitest 4 timeout signature**: `it(name, { timeout }, fn)` — options as 2nd arg, not 3rd
2. **Regex patterns**: `[\s\S]*` for newline-spanning matches (not `.*`)
3. **Temp dir isolation**: All config drift and lifecycle tests use mkdtempSync — zero blast radius
4. **State manipulation over mocking**: Direct state store manipulation for quarantine/restart scenarios
5. **Evidence-first verification**: Every scenario asserts on durable evidence records, not just HTTP status
6. **Polling with fallback**: Long-running scenarios poll with timeouts and verify whatever stages are reached
