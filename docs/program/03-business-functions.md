# Business Functions

## Objective

Model the business functions ANQR must cover, even if one human fills many roles.

# Operator / CEO

### Function
Overall authority for the ANQR program including strategy, governance, and final decision making.

### Owner
Matthew Aldous

### May be automated?
Partially.

The autonomous system may:
- prepare reports
- summarize performance
- draft strategy options
- identify risks and opportunities

### Must remain human?
Yes.

Final authority over:
- strategic direction
- financial decisions
- production approvals
- platform governance
- major product decisions

### Key recurring outputs
- weekly executive status report
- strategy updates
- program roadmap decisions
- priority directives

### Key scorecards
- platform availability
- growth trajectory
- operational stability
- development velocity
- financial sustainability
